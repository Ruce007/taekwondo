The Jung Do Kwan was founded in Seoul by [[Lee Yong-woo]]. Wikipedia and Kang Won-sik and Lee Kyong-myong give 1954, and other sources give 1956.[^1][^2][^3] Kang and Lee place it in a lottery building in Seodaemun District, with branches later opened in Masan, Ulsan, Changwon, Mokpo and Gimje.[^3]

## Lineage

Lee Yong-woo trained at the [[Chung Do Kwan]] and founded the Jung Do Kwan as its annex. The two kwans' accounts agree that the split was amicable and that the name was a variation on "Chung Do".[^1][^3][^4]

## Unification and today

The Jung Do Kwan is not among the six kwans Kang and Lee list at the 1959 founding of the Korea Taekwondo Association. Lee became a Korea Taekwondo Association director in 1963 and was among the kwan heads who signed the unification that closed the kwan system in 1978.[^1][^3] The kwan continues in Korea as a social club and follows the [[Kukkiwon]] curriculum.[^4]
