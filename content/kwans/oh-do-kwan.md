The Oh Do Kwan was co-founded by [[Choi Hong-hi]] and [[Nam Tae-hi]].[^2] Wikipedia's article on kwans lists the founding as 1955; Nam Tae-hi's article gives no year, so this page says "mid-1950s" until a firmer source is found.[^1][^2]

## Role in naming Taekwondo

The ITF says instructors of the Oh Do Kwan and the [[Chung Do Kwan]] sat on the 1955 board that approved the name Taekwon-Do.[^3] Choi, an officer in the Korean army, convened that board and later founded the [[International Taekwon-Do Federation]].[^3][^4] See [[History of Taekwondo]].
