The Chang Moo Kwan traces its origin to a Kwon Bup club at the YMCA started by [[Yun Byung-in]] in 1946.[^1][^3]

## Name and succession

Yun disappeared, and his top student Lee Nam Suk carried on his teaching.[^1] After the Korean War, Lee and Kim Soon Bae reopened the club under the name Chang Moo Kwan, which the Song Moo Kwan's records date to 1954.[^3][^4] The name is also spelled Chang Mu Kwan.

## Relationship to the Ji Do Kwan

Yun was a close associate of [[Chun Sang-sup]], and the Chang Moo Kwan is described as having grown out of Yun's YMCA club, with Lee Nam Suk among the senior students involved. See [[Ji Do Kwan]] for the related lineage.[^2]

Yun trained under Kanken Toyama in Shudokan karate and was said to have studied ch'uan-fa in Manchuria.[^2] Accounts differ on what happened to him after the Korean War; see his page.
