The Song Moo Kwan was founded by [[Ro Byung-jick]] in Kaesong. The kwan dates its founding to 20 March 1944.[^1][^2][^3] Kang Won-sik and Lee Kyong-myong give 1946 instead.[^4] The kwan's own timeline says it was re-established in Kaesong on 2 May 1946, closed on 25 June 1950 at the start of the Korean War, and reopened in Seoul on 20 September 1953.[^3]

## Origins

Its ancestor arts include Kong Soo Do and Shaolin kung fu, and the Korean Kongsoodo Association is named as an ancestor school. Ro studied Shotokan karate under Gichin Funakoshi and developed a style with more emphasis on kicking.[^1]

## Organizations today

Affiliated groups include the World Martial Arts Center and the World Song Moo Kwan Federation.[^1] The kwan's own site is songmookwan.com.
