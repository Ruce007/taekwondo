The Ji Do Kwan began as a Kong Soo Do club founded by [[Chun Sang-sup]] on 3 March 1946. The date marks the karate club, not the origin of the Chosun Yun Moo Kwan itself, which the sources describe as a long-established Kodokan judo school.[^1]

## The Yun Moo Kwan

Chun taught karate in the Kwon Bup section of the Chosun Yun Moo Kwan, which Wikipedia says was one of the few martial arts schools the Japanese occupying forces allowed to stay open.[^2] The school's name appears as Yun Moo Kwan and Yun Mu Kwan.

## After the war

After the Korean War the school was restarted under new teachers and the name Ji Do Kwan. Yun Kwae-byung was voted first headmaster by Chun's former students.[^2] [[Yun Byung-in]] was Chun's close associate, and the [[Chang Moo Kwan]] grew from his YMCA club.

## Today

The Ji Do Kwan was absorbed into the unified Korean Taekwondo system. Wikipedia says it operates in Korea today as a social fraternal order that endorses the Kukkiwon method and supports World Taekwondo.[^2]
