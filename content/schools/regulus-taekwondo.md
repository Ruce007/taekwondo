Regulus Taekwondo is a Taekwondo school at 1150 Roosevelt, Suite 100, Irvine, California 92620, serving Irvine and Orange County.[^1][^2] It has one location.[^2]

## Programs

The school says it is led by [[Kukkiwon]]-certified masters and former elite athletes.[^1] It runs Lion Cubs (ages 3.5 to 5), Lion Kings and Queens (ages 6 to 10) and Lion Teens and Adults (ages 11 and up), along with invitation-only Red Squad and Demo Team and Leadership Team programs.[^1]
