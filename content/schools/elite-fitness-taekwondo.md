Elite Fitness Taekwondo is a Taekwondo school at Woodbury Town Center, 6254 Irvine Blvd, Irvine, California 92620, near Trader Joe's. It is the second location of [[Il-Do Taekwondo]]: Il-Do's website lists it as one of the school's "two convenient locations", and the Elite Fitness website carries Il-Do's copyright and describes the tradition led by Grandmaster [[Chang Jin Kang]].[^1][^2]

## Programs and certification

The school offers family classes, Cubs (ages 3 to 4), Tigers (ages 5 to 12) and adult and teen classes, and certifies students through [[World Taekwondo]] and the [[Kukkiwon]].[^2]

## Other locations

Il-Do's original Irvine school is at Heritage Plaza, 14220 Culver Dr #D, Irvine, California 92604; see [[Il-Do Taekwondo]].[^1] Elite Fitness once had a third location at University Center in Irvine, which has since closed.
