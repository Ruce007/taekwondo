Dynamic Martial Arts is a martial arts school at 14805 Jeffrey Rd, Suite F, Irvine, California. Founded in 2004, it serves Irvine, Lake Forest and Tustin from a single location.[^1]

## Programs

The school runs kids', teen and adult martial arts classes, a transported after-school program, summer camp, birthday parties and an anti-bullying program for youth, with educational resources for parents. A business directory lists its arts as Taekwondo and Hapkido.[^1][^2]

## Recognition

The school lists an Expertise.com award, "Best Martial Arts Teachers in Irvine 2018".[^1]
