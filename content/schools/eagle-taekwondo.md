Eagle Taekwondo, also called Eagle Tae Kwon Do Academy, has served Las Vegas since 1986. It has three locations, all sharing the phone number (702) 736-1040, and serves Las Vegas, Southern Highlands, Henderson and Summerlin.[^1][^2]

## Locations

- **Jones:** 7750 S. Jones Blvd #105, Las Vegas, NV 89139, at Jones and Robindale.[^1][^3]
- **Tropicana:** 1801 E. Tropicana Ave #14, Las Vegas, NV 89119.[^1]
- **Summerlin:** 2950 S. Durango Dr. #100, Las Vegas, NV 89117.[^1]

## Founder and history

[[Bo Young Kim]] founded the academy. After his death in 2008, his two daughters continued it.[^2] The current staff includes Masters Helen Kim and Ester Kim, both 6th dan, and Masters Sabrina Brasi and Marson Lagatao, both 4th dan, along with a team of instructors and a demo team.[^2]

## Programs

Classes cover kids, teens and adults, with birthday parties at the Jones and Tropicana locations. Kids' classes start at age 7 at Jones and Tropicana and at age 4 at Summerlin.[^1][^3]
