Il-Do Taekwondo is a Taekwondo school founded by Grandmaster [[Chang Jin Kang]] in Santa Ana, California, in 1980. It later moved to Irvine, where the Irvine school opened in 1991, and the school describes itself as the first Taekwondo school established in Irvine.[^1][^3] The name "Il-Do" translates as "One Way".[^2]

## Locations

- **Heritage Plaza:** 14220 Culver Dr #D, Irvine, California 92604, near Mitsuwa. Phone (949) 551-1800.[^1][^2]
- **Elite Fitness Taekwondo, Woodbury Town Center:** 6254 Irvine Blvd, Irvine, California 92620, near Trader Joe's. Phone (949) 387-9888. Il-Do's website lists it as the second of its "two convenient locations", and it has its own page: [[Elite Fitness Taekwondo]].[^1][^4]

A third location, at University Center in Irvine, has since closed.

## Instruction and lineage

Grandmaster Kang leads the school and holds the rank of 9th degree black belt.[^2] He trained directly under [[Ro Byung-jick]], the founder of the [[Song Moo Kwan]].[^1] His page covers his competition record, military service, rank history and community work.

## University program

Kang created the first bachelor's degree program in Taekwondo at Concordia University, which the school describes as the first institution to offer such a degree outside Korea.[^2] He also taught Taekwondo as the official P.E. class at CREAN Lutheran High School in 2010.[^3] His page has the details.

## Certification

The school certifies students through [[World Taekwondo]] and the [[Kukkiwon]], and its website carries a "Kukkiwon certified" banner.[^1][^4]
