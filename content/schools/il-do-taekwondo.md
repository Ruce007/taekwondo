Il-Do Taekwondo is a Taekwondo school in Irvine, California. Its website says it was established in Orange County in 1980 and was the first Taekwondo school established in Irvine.[^1] The school translates "Il-Do" as "One Way".[^2]

> **Source note.** Everything on this page is taken from the school's own website. It has not been independently verified, and each claim below is attributed to the school.

## Location

The school's website lists a location at Heritage Plaza, 14220 Culver Dr #D, Irvine, California 92604, and describes the school as a fixture in Irvine for over 40 years.[^1][^2]

## Instruction and lineage

The school's website names Grandmaster Chang Jin Kang as its head and lists him as a 9th degree black belt.[^2] It says he "trained directly under" [[Ro Byung-jick]], the founder of the [[Song Moo Kwan]].[^1] That statement is the school's own and is the basis for the stated lineage above.

## University program

The school's website says Grandmaster Kang created the first bachelor's degree program in Taekwondo at Concordia University and describes Concordia as the first institution to offer such a degree outside Korea.[^2] This is the school's claim and is listed here as such. Independent confirmation is still to be added.
