Il-Do Taekwondo is a Taekwondo school in Irvine, California, founded by [[Chang Jin Kang]]. It was established in Orange County in 1980 and was the first Taekwondo school established in Irvine.[^1] The name "Il-Do" translates as "One Way".[^2]

## Locations

Il-Do has two locations in Irvine:

- **Heritage Plaza:** 14220 Culver Dr #D, Irvine, California 92604, near Mitsuwa.[^1][^2]
- **Elite Fitness Taekwondo, Woodbury Town Center:** 6254 Irvine Blvd, Irvine, California 92620, near Trader Joe's. The school's website presents it as the second of its "two convenient locations", and it is listed separately as [[Elite Fitness Taekwondo]].[^1][^4]

Kang's biography on the school's website dates the Santa Ana school to 1980 and the Irvine school to 1991.[^3]

## Instruction and lineage

Grandmaster [[Chang Jin Kang]] is the head of the school and holds the rank of 9th degree black belt.[^2] He trained directly under [[Ro Byung-jick]], the founder of the [[Song Moo Kwan]].[^1]

## Certification

The school certifies students through [[World Taekwondo]] and the [[Kukkiwon]], and its website carries a "Kukkiwon certified" banner.[^1][^4]

## University program

Kang created the first bachelor's degree program in Taekwondo at Concordia University, the first institution to offer such a degree outside Korea.[^2] His page has the details.
