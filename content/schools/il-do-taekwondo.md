Il-Do Taekwondo is a Taekwondo school in Irvine, California. It was established in Orange County in 1980 and was the first Taekwondo school established in Irvine.[^1] The name "Il-Do" translates as "One Way".[^2]

## Location

The school is at Heritage Plaza, 14220 Culver Dr #D, Irvine, California 92604, and has been a fixture in Irvine for over 40 years.[^1][^2]

## Instruction and lineage

Grandmaster Chang Jin Kang is the head of the school and holds the rank of 9th degree black belt.[^2] He trained directly under [[Ro Byung-jick]], the founder of the [[Song Moo Kwan]].[^1]

## University program

Grandmaster Kang created the first bachelor's degree program in Taekwondo at Concordia University, the first institution to offer such a degree outside Korea.[^2]
