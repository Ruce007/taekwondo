ATA Martial Arts Irvine, also listed as OC ATA Family Martial Arts, is a school of [[ATA Martial Arts]], formerly the American Taekwondo Association. It is at 15435 Jeffrey Road, Suite 100, Irvine, California 92618, phone 949-552-3602.[^1][^2] ATA schools teach Songahm Taekwondo, the organization's own curriculum.
