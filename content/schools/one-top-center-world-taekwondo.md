One Top Center World Taekwondo is a Taekwondo school at 9055 W Post Rd #A, Las Vegas, Nevada 89148.[^1] Its headmaster is Steban Monroy Diaz, a native of Colombia and former member of the Colombian national team.[^2]

## Athletes and events

Fox 5 Vegas reported in March 2026 that the school trained 12 students for the 2026 U.S. Open at the Westgate, had hosted athletes from Ecuador, Mexico, Korea and Chinese Taipei, and had sent students to the U.S. Open for two years.[^2]
