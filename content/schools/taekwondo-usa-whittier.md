Taekwondo USA, also listed as Tae Kwon Do USA, is a Taekwondo school at 15035 Mulberry Dr, Whittier, California 90604.[^1][^2] One directory dates it to 2001 and another to 1999.[^1][^2] The listing names World Taekwondo Federation, American Taekwondo Union and California Taekwondo United affiliations and describes belt progression to black belt plus a "Hosinsul" self-defense program.[^1]
