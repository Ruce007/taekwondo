Lone Mountain Taekwondo is a Taekwondo school at 4990 W. Craig Road, Suite 8, Las Vegas, Nevada 89130. It has one location and was opened in 1994 by [[Sue Langdon]].[^1][^2]

## Training and organizations

Training is based on the standards of the [[Kukkiwon]], and the school names the UWTA as its parent organization and a competition circuit.[^1] Its lineage is the [[Moo Duk Kwan]]: Langdon is a Moo Duk Kwan 7th dan and a member of the Moodo Taekwondo Organization.[^2]

## Programs

Classes are offered for kids, adults and families, along with a Black Belt program covering forms, fitness, self-defense and sparring.[^1]
