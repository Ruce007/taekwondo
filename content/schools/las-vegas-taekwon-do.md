Las Vegas Taekwon-Do is a Taekwon-Do school at 2651 Windmill Pkwy, Henderson, Nevada 89074, in the Las Vegas area. It is led by Grand Master Joseph Bosse, a 9th dan in the USTF, with instructors Cassie Bosse (4th dan) and Anjelo Vigilia (3rd dan). Master Gary Bosse trained the first two from childhood.[^1]

## Programs

Classes run for Little Tigers (ages 4 to 6), families (6 and up), teens and adults (13 and up), and adults only (18 and up), with private lessons, free trial classes and monthly in-school tournaments.[^1]
