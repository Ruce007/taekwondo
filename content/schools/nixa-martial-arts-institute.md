Nixa Martial Arts Institute is a martial arts school at 1501 Corporate Dr, Suite B7, Ladera Ranch, California 92694, serving Ladera Ranch, Rancho Mission Viejo, Las Flores and Rancho Santa Margarita.[^1][^2] The school's website does not state its founding year.

## Locations

- **Ladera Ranch:** 1501 Corporate Dr, Suite B7, Ladera Ranch, CA 92694. Phone (949) 422-8683.[^1]
- **Whittier:** 14828 Whittier Blvd, Whittier, CA 90605. Phone (949) 322-5274.[^3]

## Programs

The school offers Little Tiger classes (ages 4 to 5.5), kids' martial arts (ages 5.5 and up), adult and family classes, a Leadership and Demo Team and birthday parties.[^1]

## Owner

[[Yong Jin Park]] owns the school and holds World Taekwondo international referee credentials in both poomsae and sparring.[^2]
