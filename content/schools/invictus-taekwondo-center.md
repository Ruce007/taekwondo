Invictus Taekwondo Center, also called MAC Las Vegas Taekwondo, is listed at 8090 Blue Diamond Rd, Suite 150, Las Vegas, Nevada 89113.[^1] A local magazine article names Glenn "Cody" Smith, known as Master Cody, as owner and headmaster, and says the school was founded in 2016 and teaches a World Taekwondo or Kukkiwon-based curriculum for students from about age 4 to 70.[^2]
