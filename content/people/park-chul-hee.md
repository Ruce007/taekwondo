Park Chul-hee was born in 1932 and died in April 2016. He co-founded the [[Kang Duk Won]] with [[Hong Jong-pyo]] and led it as its second kwan jang.[^1][^2] He taught Taekwondo to Vietnamese troops from 1966 and visited Michigan in 1970, according to the kwan.[^1]
