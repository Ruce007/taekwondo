Lee Won-kuk founded the [[Chung Do Kwan]] in Korea around 1944.[^1] Many later Taekwondo leaders trace their training to his school.

## Training

Wikipedia reports that Lee learned taekkyon in a Seoul neighbourhood, studied karate under Gichin Funakoshi at Chuo University in Japan, where he earned a fourth dan in Shotokan, and trained in kung fu in China. He also had a background in law, which led to a teaching post at the Korean Police Academy.[^1]

## Students and disputes

Early students included Son Duk-sung, who became head of the kwan, and Uhm Woon-kyu, who succeeded him in 1959.[^1] Lee and [[Hwang Kee]] gave conflicting accounts of whether Hwang had trained at Lee's kwan. Lee said he had; Hwang said his only teacher was Yang Kuk Jin.[^2]

Birth and death dates are not yet verified from a source used on this site, so none are given here.
