Lee Won-kuk founded the [[Chung Do Kwan]] in Korea around 1944.[^1] Many later Taekwondo leaders trace their training to his school.

## Training

Lee was born in Seoul on 13 April 1907. He studied law at Chuo University in Tokyo, where he learned karate and reached fourth dan.[^3] Accounts say he trained under Gichin Funakoshi for five years, although Funakoshi's son Gigo is considered the more likely instructor. Before 1945 he worked for the Japanese government in Manchuria.[^3] Other accounts add taekkyon training in Seoul, kung fu study in China and a teaching post at the Korean Police Academy.[^1]

## Students and disputes

Early students included Son Duk-sung, who became head of the kwan, and Uhm Woon-kyu, who succeeded him in 1959.[^1] Lee and [[Hwang Kee]] gave conflicting accounts of whether Hwang had trained at Lee's kwan. Lee said he had; Hwang said his only teacher was Yang Kuk Jin.[^2]
