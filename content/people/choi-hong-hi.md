Choi Hong-hi was born in 1918 and died on 15 June 2002 in Pyongyang.[^1][^4] The ITF gives his birthday as 9 November by the lunar calendar, which is 22 December in the Western calendar.[^4] He was an officer in the Korean army, co-founded the [[Oh Do Kwan]] with [[Nam Tae-hi]], and founded the [[International Taekwon-Do Federation]] on 22 March 1966.[^1][^3]

## Naming Taekwon-Do

His organization named the art Taekwon-Do on 11 April 1955.[^1] The ITF says he conceived the term in the autumn and winter of 1954.[^4] The ITF calls him "the Father of Taekwon-Do" and says he convened the 1955 board that approved the name.[^2] Other sources give different dates and a different distribution of credit (see [[History of Taekwondo]]), so this site treats the title as the ITF's own description.

## Exile

In 1972 he went into exile in Canada after the South Korean government refused to let his organization teach Taekwondo in North Korea.[^1] After the ITF's headquarters moved to Vienna, he led it until his death in 2002; the federation then divided into separate groups.[^3]
