Lee Yong-woo trained at the [[Chung Do Kwan]] and founded the [[Jung Do Kwan]] in Seoul in the 1950s.[^1] He became a Korea Taekwondo Association director in 1963 and died in Seoul in August 2006.[^1][^2]
