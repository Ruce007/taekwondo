Master Yong Jin Park is the owner of [[Nixa Martial Arts Institute]] in Ladera Ranch, California. He began martial arts as a child and opened his own school to pass on what he learned.[^1] His credentials include [[World Taekwondo]] international referee certification in both poomsae and sparring.[^1] The school's website gives no rank or lineage.
