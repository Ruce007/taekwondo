Lee Kyo-yoon (1930 to 2017) trained at the Chosun Yun Moo Kwan under [[Chun Sang-sup]] and founded the [[Han Moo Kwan]] in the 1950s.[^1][^2] He held posts in the Korea Taekwondo Association from 1961 and served on Kukkiwon testing and advisory bodies in the 2000s.[^1]
