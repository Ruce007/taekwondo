Chun Sang-sup is listed as the founder of the club that became the [[Ji Do Kwan]]. The club was founded in 1946 as a Kong Soo Do club, on 3 March or 3 May depending on the source.[^1][^3]

## Background

Chun studied Shotokan karate under Gichin Funakoshi at Takushoku University and taught karate at the Kwon Bup section of the Chosun Yun Moo Kwan, which was a judo school that the occupying Japanese forces allowed to stay open.[^2]

## Fate

He vanished during the Korean War and was never heard from again. After the war, the school was restarted under the name Ji Do Kwan, with Yun Kwae-byung voted as first headmaster by Chun's former students.[^2] [[Yun Byung-in]] was a close associate of his.
