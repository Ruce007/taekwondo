Ro Byung-jick was born on 3 July 1919 in Kaesong, in what is now North Korea, and died on 9 September 2015 in Minneapolis, Minnesota.[^3] He founded the [[Song Moo Kwan]] in Kaesong, and the kwan dates its founding to 20 March 1944.[^1][^2][^3]

## Background

Ro began studying Shotokan karate under Gichin Funakoshi in December 1936 and later developed a style with more emphasis on kicking.[^1][^3] The Song Moo Kwan describes him as a founder of modern Taekwondo; that title is the kwan's own, since Taekwondo has no single universally recognized founder (see [[History of Taekwondo]]).

## Spelling

His name appears in English as Ro Byung-jick, Byung Jik Ro and Byung Jick Ro. This site uses the first form.
