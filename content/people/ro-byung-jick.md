Ro Byung-jick (1919 to 2015) founded the [[Song Moo Kwan]] in Kaesong, in what is now North Korea. Wikipedia gives the founding date as 20 March 1944.[^1][^2]

## Background

Wikipedia describes Ro as a Shotokan black belt under Gichin Funakoshi who developed a style with more emphasis on kicking.[^1] The Song Moo Kwan describes him as a founder of modern Taekwondo; this site treats that as the kwan's own description, because Taekwondo has no single universally recognized founder (see [[History of Taekwondo]]).

## Spelling

His name appears in English as Ro Byung-jick, Byung Jik Ro and Byung Jick Ro. This site uses the first form.
