Master Sue Langdon is the owner and chief instructor of [[Lone Mountain Taekwondo]] in Las Vegas. She began training in 1986, returned to it in 1989, earned her first-degree black belt in 1991 and opened her school in 1994. She holds a 7th dan in the [[Moo Duk Kwan]] lineage and is a member of the Moodo Taekwondo Organization.[^1]

Her listed past affiliations include the ATA, ITC, AAU, USAT and UWTA. She is a Level 3 certified judge and regional chief of tournaments, and her listed achievements include five ITC national sparring championships and the 2019 Taekwondo Poomsae National Championship in forms.[^1]
