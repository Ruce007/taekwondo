tkd.wiki is a reference for the history, lineages, organizations and schools of Taekwondo. It is being rebuilt from the ground up as a long-term knowledge base.

## What it is for

- A **reference library** of history, kwans, founders, notable teachers and organizations, with sources shown on each page.
- A **free school directory** where visitors can find and compare schools by lineage, location and training goals.
- Later, a **community layer**, including reviews and member profile pages, once the editorial foundation is solid.

Core access and participation will remain free. Optional paid tools for school owners may be added later, but they will never decide what a page says or how a school is ranked.

## Where the project is now

The site is in an early, curated stage. The editors write and review every page, and pages are marked **Provisional** until their facts have been checked against primary or independent sources. The first pages concern the original kwans, the main international organizations and the people who shaped them.

## Who runs it

The site is run by a small editorial team that is not yet named publicly. Editors may have personal ties to schools or lineages that appear here. Where that could matter, entries state their sources and attribute claims to the people who made them. See the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
