Taekwondo is a Korean martial art and combat sport that emphasizes kicking techniques. It is practised in many styles and under several organizations.[^1] World Taekwondo reports 215 national member associations as of 2026.[^2]

## Overview

Modern Taekwondo did not come from a single founder. It developed after 1945 through a group of Korean martial arts schools called [kwans](/kwans/), which later worked toward a shared name and, eventually, separate international organizations. The [[History of Taekwondo]] article explains how this happened and where the sources disagree.

## Styles and organizations

Two international traditions are most visible:

- **World Taekwondo (WT)** governs the Olympic-style sport and works closely with the [[Kukkiwon]], the technical centre in Seoul.[^2]
- **The International Taekwon-Do Federation (ITF)**, founded by [[Choi Hong-hi]] in 1966, uses its own patterns and rules. Since 2002 several separate groups have claimed its heritage.

National federations and independent schools sit under, alongside or outside these bodies.
