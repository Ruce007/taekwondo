Taekwondo first appeared at the Olympic Games as a demonstration sport in 1988 in Seoul and again in 1992. It became a full medal sport at the 2000 Summer Olympics in Sydney.[^1]

## Recognition milestones

- On 17 July 1980 the International Olympic Committee recognized the World Taekwondo Federation, now [[World Taekwondo]].[^2]
- On 4 September 1994 the IOC adopted Taekwondo as an official sport of the 2000 Summer Olympics.[^2]

Olympic Taekwondo uses World Taekwondo's rules.

## Events

The Olympic programme has eight events, four for men and four for women.[^1]

| Men | Women |
|---|---|
| Under 58 kg | Under 49 kg |
| Under 68 kg | Under 57 kg |
| Under 80 kg | Under 67 kg |
| Over 80 kg | Over 67 kg |

A National Olympic Committee may qualify one athlete per weight category since 2016. Before 2012 the limit was two men and two women per committee.[^1]
