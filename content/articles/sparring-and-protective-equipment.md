## Scoring under World Taekwondo rules

In World Taekwondo competition, valid points are recorded by an electronic scoring system built into the protective equipment. Hits to the body protector (*hogu*) score points, and kicks to the head score more than body kicks.[^1] Scoring values have changed over time, so check the current World Taekwondo competition rules before relying on a number.

## Protective equipment

- **Hogu:** chest protector covering the torso.
- **Headgear:** padded helmet protecting the head and face.
- **Forearm and shin guards:** padding for the limbs that block and kick most often.
- **Groin and mouth guards:** standard protection in contact sports.
- **Optional padding:** instep guards, hand protectors and other items, depending on the rules and level.

## Other rule sets

ITF-style sparring is generally lighter in contact and uses different scoring and protective equipment. Rules vary between the ITF groups and between schools.
