The word **Taekwondo** (Korean 태권도, written 跆拳道 in Chinese characters) is commonly analyzed as three parts:[^1]

| Part | Characters | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Tae | 跆 | To strike or stomp with the foot |
| Kwon | 拳 | The fist, or to strike with the hand |
| Do | 道 | The way, path or discipline |

It is therefore often translated as "the way of the foot and the fist".

## An earlier name: Tae Soo Do

An earlier name, Tae Soo Do, used the characters 跆 (tae), 手 (su, "hand") and 道 (do). [[Choi Hong-hi]] replaced 手 with 拳 (kwon, "fist") to form the name Taekwondo.[^1] The page [[History of Taekwondo]] covers the disputed dates for when the name was adopted.

## Spellings

The art appears in English as Taekwondo, Tae Kwon Do and, in the ITF tradition, Taekwon-Do. The spellings refer to the same word. This site uses "Taekwondo" except where an organization's own name or usage is being quoted.
