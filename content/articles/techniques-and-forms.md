Taekwondo is mainly a striking art. Kicks delivered from a mobile stance are its best-known feature. Hand techniques, blocks and stances are also taught from the first lessons.

## Technique groups

- **Kicks:** front kick, roundhouse kick, side kick, back kick, axe kick, hook kick, and various jumping and turning kicks.
- **Hand techniques:** straight punches, knife-hand strikes, ridge-hand strikes, back-fist strikes and palm-heel strikes.
- **Blocks:** low, middle and high blocks, inward and outward blocks, and other deflections.
- **Stances:** walking, front, back, sitting and sparring stances.

## Patterns and forms

Taekwondo teaches fixed sequences of techniques. They are called **poomsae** in the World Taekwondo tradition, **tul** in the ITF tradition and **hyung** in some other schools. The official ITF syllabus contains 24 patterns.[^1]

## Sparring

Sparring, called *kyorugi* or *matsogi*, is part of training in most schools. Rules differ between World Taekwondo, ITF groups and independent schools. See [[Sparring and protective equipment]].
