USA Taekwondo (USATKD) is the national governing body for the sport of Taekwondo in the United States.[^1] It is recognized by the United States Olympic and Paralympic Committee and by [[World Taekwondo]], of which it is a member national association.[^2] The Olympic committee reaffirmed it as the national governing body on 3 July 2024, a recertification that runs through the 2028 Los Angeles Olympic Games.[^3]

## Relationship to World Taekwondo

At World Taekwondo's General Assembly in September 2024, USA Taekwondo was named the top member national association in governance. The top five were the United States, Korea, Italy, France and Great Britain.[^2]

## Relationship to the Kukkiwon

On 9 May 2024 USA Taekwondo and the [[Kukkiwon]] announced an agreement making USATKD the Kukkiwon's primary partner in the United States. The stated goals include education courses and competitions, growing the community of Poom and Dan holders, and building standardized systems.[^1]

## Other organizations

USA Taekwondo is separate from independent organizations such as [[ATA Martial Arts]], which teaches its own curriculum. See [[Major Taekwondo organizations compared]].
