World Taekwondo (WT) is the international federation for the Olympic form of Taekwondo. It was established on 28 May 1973 as the World Taekwondo Federation at an inaugural meeting held at the [[Kukkiwon]] with 35 representatives.[^1]

## Olympic recognition

The International Olympic Committee recognized the federation on 17 July 1980. On 4 September 1994 Taekwondo was adopted as an official sport of the 2000 Summer Olympics. See [[Taekwondo at the Olympics]].[^1]

## Name

In June 2017 the World Taekwondo Federation was renamed World Taekwondo, partly because its former abbreviation clashed with an internet slang term.[^1]

## Membership and leadership

Wikipedia lists 215 national member associations as of 2026, of which 214 are active and one is suspended. Choue Chung-won has led the federation since 2004.[^1]

## Relationship to other bodies

WT works closely with the [[Kukkiwon]], and it is distinct from the [[International Taekwon-Do Federation]] and the groups that claim its heritage. Competition rules are summarized under [[Sparring and protective equipment]].
